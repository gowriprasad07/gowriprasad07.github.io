Who I am

I've been on the security assessment team at Zoho since 2022. Day to day that means web application penetration testing across our products, secure code review, and vulnerability research — sitting with an application until its logic makes sense, then finding the place where that logic disagrees with itself.

A good part of the job is bug bounty triage: reproducing what a researcher reported, deciding whether it's real, scoring it, tracing it back to a root cause, and then staying with it until the fix actually holds. That work has taught me more than my own testing did. Reproducing someone else's finding forces you to understand a system you didn't choose, and often enough the more interesting bug is sitting next to the one that was reported.

The rest is scripting. I write Python and Bash to take the repetitive parts out of an assessment, and maintain reusable test cases so the same checks don't get rebuilt from scratch every time. Automation handles the predictable half. Access control, authentication flows, and business logic still need somebody who understands what the application is for.
